Project Milestones

Milestone 1Project Charter
📅 13 March 2026 ✓ Completed ▼

The project charter defined the scope of Bhasha — automated early-literacy monitoring in Sinhala and Tamil — along with each member's component, team roles and the initial project plan.

Deliverables: Project charter document

Milestone 2Project Proposal
📅 17 March 2026 ✓ Completed ▼

The proposal presented the early-literacy problem in Sri Lanka, the literature review and research gap, the main and sub-objectives, and the proposed handwriting and speech analysis methodology for each of the four components.

Deliverables: Proposal report, proposal presentation

Milestone 3Progress Presentation 1
📅 12 May 2026 ✓ Completed ▼

Progress Presentation 1 demonstrated data collection from Sinhala- and Tamil-medium schools, the preprocessing pipelines and the initial implementation of each component.

Deliverables: Presentation slides, checklist, system demo

Milestone 4Progress Presentation 2
📅 1 September 2026 ✓ Completed ▼

Progress Presentation 2 demonstrated the trained models — TrOCR, spelling and grammar correction, handwriting-quality classifiers, Whisper speech recognition and fluency profiling — with evaluation results.

Deliverables: Presentation slides, checklist, system demo

Milestone 5Research Paper
📅 4 September 2026 ✓ Completed ▼

The research paper “Early Literacy Monitoring through Handwriting and Speech Analysis in Sinhala and Tamil” presenting the integrated framework and its evaluation.

Deliverables: Research paper

Milestone 6Final Presentation
📅 21 October 2026 ⏳ Upcoming ▼

Presentation of the complete Bhasha framework with results, evaluation, limitations and future work, including a demonstration of the system.

Deliverables: Final presentation slides, system demonstration

Milestone 7Viva
📅 21 October 2026 ⏳ Upcoming ▼

Individual oral examination of each member on their component, together with a demonstration of this research website.

Deliverables: Individual viva, research website demonstration

Milestone 8Final Report
📅 28 October 2026 ⏳ Upcoming ▼

Submission of the final group report and the four individual component reports documenting the complete research.

Deliverables: Final group report and 4 individual component reports

Abstract

About the Project

BHASHA is a bilingual early-literacy monitoring platform created to make the assessment of young learners more meaningful, consistent, and accessible in Sinhala- and Tamil-medium classrooms. Designed for primary school learners aged 8 to 10 , the project responds to a practical classroom challenge: teachers often need to review exercise books, listen to individual reading, and rely on separate observations to understand how each child is progressing. BHASHA brings these different forms of evidence together by analysing handwriting and oral reading within one platform, helping to identify spelling and grammatical difficulties, handwriting-quality issues, reading errors, fluency patterns, and areas that may require further practice, while presenting feedback in a clear and child-friendly form.

Developed by research group R26-DS-009 at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology, BHASHA combines machine learning, language processing, handwriting analysis, speech analysis, learner profiling, and progress monitoring to create a more complete picture of early literacy development.

Evaluated on real handwriting and speech from over 700 students in Colombo and Batticaloa districts, the platform gives learners, teachers and parents one integrated way to identify literacy difficulties, monitor performance and receive personalised feedback and practice support.

Why Bhasha

What Makes BHASHA Unique

Built on real children, real classrooms, and real exercise books — not borrowed datasets.

1

Built on Real Local Child Data

Handwriting collected from Grade 3–5 students' exercise books in Colombo (Sinhala) and Batticaloa (Tamil), not borrowed from adult datasets.

2

Two Languages, One Workflow

Sinhala and Tamil each get their own recogniser, spelling rules and feedback, inside the same platform.

3

Reads Real Exercise Books

Page cleaning copes with ruled lines, shadows and uneven photos, so a teacher can photograph a normal page.

4

Listens in Real Classrooms

A Sinhala reading-transcription model stays accurate with school background noise, so reading can be recorded in a normal classroom.

5

Accurate Speech Transcription

Speech transcription for Sinhala and Tamil, two low-resource languages, with high accuracy.

6

Accurate Handwriting Recognition

Child handwriting recognition for Sinhala and Tamil, two low-resource languages, built on local child data.

7

Assesses How a Child Writes, Not Only What

Handwriting quality is assessed from the same page, alongside the recognised text, so a teacher sees both what was written and how well.

8

Child-Friendly Feedback & Follow-up Lessons

Explanations are written in the child's own script and a gentle tone, with targeted practice for each error type found.

9

Corrects Without Damaging Meaning

Spelling uses four independent word resources and a confidence gate; in testing, a plain dictionary checker made text worse, while the gate kept real words intact.

10

Parents and Teachers Follow Progress

A parent creates a child account; writing, handwriting quality, reading errors and fluency are tracked together over 30 days, as a trend, not a single score.

From Problem to Outcome

The research journey in three steps.

❓

The Problem

Literacy assessment for young children in Sri Lanka is still done manually, with teachers having no automated way to catch spelling, handwriting, or reading-fluency difficulties early — especially in Sinhala and Tamil, which have almost no supporting technology.

→
⚙️

Our Approach

Analyse children’s handwriting, spelling, and oral reading using trained ML models — handwriting-quality scoring, NLP-based spelling correction, and speech-recognition-driven fluency profiling (hesitation, accuracy, sentence-length analysis) — across Sinhala and Tamil.

→
🌱

Key Outcome

A bilingual platform that detects literacy difficulties across handwriting, spelling, and reading fluency, and gives teachers/students clear, actionable feedbacks

Key Contributions

Research Components

Four components, each led by one member of the research group.

1

Handwriting Text Recognition, Spelling & Grammar Correction

A fine-tuned TrOCR model reads children's handwriting; a hybrid spelling module and Gemini LLM correct errors with child-friendly explanations.

2

Handwriting Quality Analysis

Language-specific ML models rate handwriting quality from structural features and give explainable, personalised improvement tips.

3

Speech Recognition & Error Identification

Fine-tuned Whisper models transcribe oral reading and measure reading errors, accuracy and reading behaviour.

4

Reading Fluency Profiling & Activity Recommendation

Whisper transcription and acoustic analysis feed a Random Forest Model that profiles each reader and recommends practice activities for their weakest skills.
